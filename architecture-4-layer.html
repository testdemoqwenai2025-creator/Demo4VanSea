4 The 5 New Pieces (October 2026 Alignment)

1

WebGPU Digital Twin

Commits our WASM surfaces to WebGPU specifically — the W3C standard for GPU-accelerated browser rendering. Y2: supply-chain graph + corridor map. Y3: carbon-ledger route optimizer.

2

Bi-National Query Mesh

Formal cross-jurisdictional query abstraction layer. Every cross-border query carries a residency_guarantee field (hash-anchored). Y2: formalized. Y3: federation-ready.

3

Intermodal Hedging Engine

Truck ↔ rail ↔ marine mode-hedging. Per-shipment recommendations ("reroute from BNSF rail to marine vessel, save $1,750 but add 3 days transit"). Y2 launch.

4

Smart Contracts

On-chain executable logic on Polygon (EVM-compatible, Solidity). 3 use cases: revenue-share payouts, compliance verification, carbon credit issuance. Y3 production.

5

Entity Identity Mesh

Cross-domain entity ID resolver service. Maintains equivalence graph: "this tender = this contract = this bid = this shipment." Y3 launch alongside Module A (Nexus Terminal).

+

Carbon MRV Ledger (renamed)

Was "Dynamic Carbon Ledger." Renamed to make the V (Verification) component explicit. Every carbon calculation verified against the provenance ledger's hash chain before being reported.

6 What This Diagram Doesn't Show (We Have)

Three things in our architecture that the 4-layer diagram omits:

  • Provenance ledger with monthly blockchain anchoring — the 20-year-moat commitment. Diagram's "Temporal Knowledge" implies this but doesn't make it explicit. 03-data-mesh-event-driven-architecture.md §6.
  • Indigenous Data Governance Council with veto power — the 4th-pillar moat. Diagram's "Sovereign" nods at this but doesn't make the governance body explicit. 11-data-governance-ethical-framework.md §4.
  • Risk mitigation matrix with Partnership risk as a 5th category — diagram is purely architectural; doesn't show risk surfaces. 13-risk-mitigation-strategy.md §6.
