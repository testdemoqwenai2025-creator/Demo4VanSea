Dear Members of the Muckleshoot Tribal Council,

I am writing to respectfully propose a partnership between the Muckleshoot Indian Tribe and AIBizVan360, a regional intelligence platform serving the Cascadia bioregion (Washington, Oregon, and British Columbia).

**What AIBizVan360 Is**

AIBizVan360 is a free, public platform that aggregates environmental, economic, and infrastructure data from taxpayer-funded public sources. The platform is live at https://testdemoqwenai2025-creator.github.io/Demo4VanSea/ and is used by researchers, students, policymakers, and citizens across the region.

**Why I Am Approaching the Muckleshoot Indian Tribe**

The Muckleshoot people have stewarded the lands and waters of the Duwamish-Green-White-Puyallup river systems since time immemorial. Your traditional territory covers the most populated region of Cascadia — the Seattle-Tacoma-Auburn metropolitan area. Any regional environmental or economic platform that covers this territory without Muckleshoot perspectives is incomplete.

I have built the architecture for an indigenous data sovereignty layer in the platform, but I have intentionally left it unpopulated. I believe that indigenous data should be governed by indigenous nations — not extracted by platforms. I am seeking a partnership to co-design this layer with the Tribe.

**What I Am Proposing**

I am requesting the opportunity to meet with the Tribal Council or the Tribe's cultural and environmental staff to discuss whether this partnership aligns with Muckleshoot's priorities. Specifically, I am seeking:

1. **Listening sessions** — 3-6 months of meetings with Tribal cultural and environmental staff to understand what the Tribe would want shared (if anything), what is confidential, and how the Tribe wants to be represented.

2. **Co-design of data governance protocol** — the Tribe defines what data is shared, how it's presented, who sees it, and can revoke access at any time. AIBizVan360 builds the infrastructure; the Tribe holds the keys.

3. **Funded partnership** — I have budgeted $150,000-$250,000 for this work, which would be paid to the Tribe for their time and expertise. This is not payment for data; it is compensation for the Tribe's partnership in co-designing the platform's indigenous data layer.

**What I Am NOT Proposing**

I want to be clear about what I am not asking for:

- I am not asking the Tribe to give me data without consent
- I am not asking to publish confidential cultural information
- I am not asking to use Muckleshoot data for commercial purposes without explicit Tribal Council approval
- I am not asking to feature Muckleshoot as a "diversity checkbox" — the partnership is substantive or it does not happen

**The Platform's Commitments**

AIBizVan360 has six permanent commitments that I will not violate:

1. Public data only — all data is from taxpayer-funded sources, freely accessible
2. Provenance for every claim — every data point traces to source + timestamp + cryptographic hash
3. Indigenous data sovereignty — each nation governs their own data
4. No individual PII — no personal emails, home addresses, or phone numbers
5. Free tier forever — core value is free for founders, students, citizens
6. Transparent methodology — every scoring decision is public + versioned
