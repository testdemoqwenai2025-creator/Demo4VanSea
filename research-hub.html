📧 How to Use This Page

Each university card includes a "Email Research Office" button that opens your email client with a pre-filled subject line. Each funding opportunity has a "Apply →" link to the official program page. For student tools, click "Open Tool →" to launch. For datasets, "Access API →" links to the API documentation.

Tip for students: When emailing a research office, mention your year, major, and specific research interest. Program officers respond best to focused inquiries.

For researchers: NSF Award Search and NSERC databases are linked in the Datasets tab. Use these to identify potential collaborators and active grants before reaching out.
