## Follow-up Strategy

**Week 1**: Send initial emails (this document)
**Week 2**: Follow-up email if no response ("following up on my note from last week — would 15 minutes work?")
**Week 3**: LinkedIn message to specific person if email unacknowledged
**Week 4**: Phone call to reception if still no response
**Week 5+**: Move on — 10% response rate is good for cold outreach

**For responses**: Offer a 30-min demo. Have the live preview open. Focus on their specific interest (not your full pitch). End with a clear ask (partnership meeting, funding conversation, pilot project).

**Track in a CRM** (even a spreadsheet): org, contact, date sent, response, next step.
