Recording tips

  • Audio first. Record narration clean, then re-record screen if needed. Loom lets you overdub screen separately.
  • Pause 1 second at every scene change. Helps viewers track where they are.
  • Mouse moves slowly. Sudden cursor jumps look unprofessional. Use smooth motion.
  • Don't read verbatim. Use the spoken column as a guide, not a script. Natural delivery beats robotic.
  • If you flub, just restart the sentence. Loom's trim tool makes editing painless.
  • After recording: rename to cascadia-os-2min-demo.mp4, upload to Loom, set thumbnail to the moment the gauge shows 72%. Embed in outreach emails with subject line: "2-min demo: Cascadia procurement, AI-drafted bids".
