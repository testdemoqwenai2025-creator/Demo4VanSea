🤖 AI RESOURCE INTELLIGENCE
AI-Powered Resource Discovery and Verification
AIBizVan360 employs an AI-driven resource intelligence pipeline that continuously crawls, classifies, and ranks 200+ data sources across the corridor. Each source is verified for data quality (accuracy, recency, methodology), accessibility (API availability, licensing), and relevance (corridor-specific coverage). The system uses NLP models trained on 5 years of corridor policy documents to automatically detect new regulatory filings, economic releases, and research publications. A confidence score (0-100) is assigned to each resource, with sources scoring below 60 flagged for manual review. The resource index is updated weekly, with real-time alerts for high-impact changes such as new trade agreement provisions, major policy shifts, or significant economic data revisions. This ensures AIBizVan360’s intelligence modules always reference the most current, reliable, and actionable data available for the Cascadia corridor.
