230+ satellites in orbit · 737 MAX assembled in Renton · Paine Field longest PNW runway

Aerospace

Boeing Everett is the world's largest building by volume — final assembly for the 747, 777, and 787 Dreamliner happens here, just 35 miles north of Seattle. Blue Origin (Kent, WA) and SpaceX's Pacific Northwest partnerships anchor the commercial space corridor. The sector generates 136,000 direct jobs across Cascadia and contributes $42B to regional GDP. The corridor also hosts UPS's aircraft fleet at Louisville-Kent hub and Alaska Airlines' Seattle-Tacoma headquarters. Cascadia is the only North American region with end-to-end aerospace capability: design (Boeing), launch (Blue Origin), operations (Alaska Airlines), and MRO (multiple facilities at Paine Field).

Biotech

The Cascadia biotech corridor spans Vancouver BC (UBC's Centre for Molecular Medicine and Therapeutics), Seattle (Allen Institute for Cell Science, Fred Hutchinson Cancer Research Center, Seattle Cancer Care Alliance), and Portland (OHSU Knight Cancer Institute). Combined annual research budget exceeds $2.9B. Recent wins: Juno Therapeutics (Seattle, CAR-T cell therapy, acquired by Celgene for $9B); Zymeworks (Vancouver, bispecific antibody platform, $1.9B market cap); SpringWorks Therapeutics (Portland spin-out from Pfizer). The corridor specializes in oncology, immunology, and rare diseases — a sharp contrast to the Bay Area's broader biotech focus.

Sector 03 · Clean Energy

Clean Energy

Hydropower backbone + wind + solar + emerging geothermal

BC Hydro 82% clean grid · 1.2 GW PSE wind · Grand Coulee 6.8 GW capacity

▸ Interactive · click to load · PNG lazy on scroll
Clean Energy

hover turbines · hover solar array · hover dam

Cascadia commands the cleanest grid in North America — BC Hydro delivers 82% clean electricity to British Columbia (mostly hydro from the Peace River and Columbia River systems). Washington's grid is 70% clean (Grand Coulee + Chief Joseph + multiple dams on the Columbia). Oregon's grid is 55% clean and growing rapidly thanks to wind in the Columbia Gorge (1.2 GW Puget Sound Energy fleet) and eastern Oregon solar (580 MW installed, 22% YoY growth). The emerging opportunity is geothermal — the Cascadia Subduction Zone's volcanic belt (Mt. Baker, Mt. St. Helens, Mt. Hood, Mt. Shasta) offers world-class enhanced geothermal systems potential. BC's Newberry Volcano and Oregon's Glass Buttes are flagship US Department of Energy pilot sites.

Creative

Vancouver is the 4th-largest film and TV production center in North America ("Hollywood North") with $3B+ annual industry revenue — driven by favorable currency, generous tax credits, and proximity to LA. Portland anchors animation through LAIKA (Oscar-nominated Coraline, Kubo and the Two Strings, Missing Link) and a robust stop-motion tradition. Seattle hosts Bungie (Destiny, Halo), Valve (Half-Life, Portal), and Pokémon's Western HQ. The corridor produces 2,500+ creative technology jobs annually. The corridor's VFX cluster (MPC Vancouver, Image Engine, Sony Pictures Imageworks) handles feature film visual effects for global blockbusters including Marvel and Star Wars franchises.

Sector 05 · Maritime

Maritime

Container ports + bulk cargo + inland barge + shipbuilding

Vancouver BC 4M TEU/year · Seattle 3.5M TEU · Columbia River 50M+ tonnes

▸ Interactive · click to load · PNG lazy on scroll
Maritime

hover cargo ship · hover gantry crane · hover tugboat

Cascadia has the deepest natural ports on the North American Pacific coast. Port of Vancouver, BC handles 4M TEU containers per year (Canada's largest port, 170M+ tonnes total cargo) — the closest North American port to Asia with two-day transit time savings versus LA/Long Beach. Port of Seattle (3.5M TEU/year) specializes in Alaska-bound cargo and cruise ships. Port of Portland handles Columbia River barge traffic (50M+ tonnes/year of wheat, forest products, and minerals) flowing from as far inland as Lewiston, Idaho. Vigor Shipyards (Seattle) is the largest non-navy shipbuilder on the West Coast. The corridor is the US West Coast's primary grain export gateway — 25% of US wheat exports flow through Cascadia ports.

Tech & AI

Cascadia is the second-largest AI/ML research cluster in North America (behind only the Bay Area). Microsoft Research (Redmond, 1,000+ researchers) is partnered with OpenAI on the Stargate compute buildout. Allen Institute for AI (AI2) (Seattle) publishes open NLP and computer vision research (Semantic Scholar, AllenNLP, MosaicML prior to its acquisition). Amazon's Machine Learning lab (Seattle) powers Alexa, recommendation systems, and AWS AI services. UBC's Vector Institute affiliate and Quantum Matter Institute anchor Canada's AI research. The corridor produces 42,000 STEM graduates annually across UW + UBC + OHSU + 20+ regional colleges. AI/ML job growth is +34% YoY — fastest-growing skill segment. The 12,000 unfilled AI positions is the single largest constraint on regional growth — the HSR corridor addresses this by merging Seattle and Vancouver labor markets.
