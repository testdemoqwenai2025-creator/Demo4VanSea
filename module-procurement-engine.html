Module B — Procurement & Gazette Engine (the Wedge)

The Year-1 wedge. Semantic RFP matching, auto-compliance matrix generator, regulatory change impact simulator. Free tier + $99/bid-draft add-on.

Phase 1 wedgeProcurementComplianceFree tier

1 Purpose

This IS the Year-1 wedge. The OS's wedge-into-platform play — solve one acute, recurring, monetizable pain for one user type (procurement officers in Cascadia mid-sized contractors) exceptionally well.

Phase 1 (Months 1-6) — free + pay-per-bid-draft at $99

Free tier: 3 tenders/day, 5 compliance matrices/month, 3 saved searches, 30-day historical data. Pay-per-bid-draft at $99/draft bridges revenue to Phase 2. Target: 500 DAU + 200 paid bid drafts/month by end of Phase 1 = ~$20K MRR.

2 Key Features

2.1 Semantic RFP Matching

Users upload their company's "Capability Statement." The AI continuously scans SAM.gov, BC Bid, WA Commerce, OR Buys, and municipal portals. Daily digest: "3 new tenders match your profile at 85%+ match confidence."

Refinement — "match confidence" not "win probability"

The AI cannot predict win probability because it cannot see (a) the user's bid quality, (b) competitor pricing, (c) relationship capital with the contracting officer. The AI can predict match confidence: how well the tender's NAICS, size standard, geography, set-aside flags, and capability requirements align with the user's declared capability statement. More honest, more defensible against liability if a bid loses.

2.3 Regulatory Change Impact Simulator

When a new rule is published in the Canada Gazette, US Federal Register, WA State Register, OR Bulletin, or BC Gazette, the OS:

  1. Ingests the new rule into the regulations domain (with provenance hash)
  2. Maps the rule to affected NAICS codes
  3. For each subscribed company in those NAICS codes, simulates the financial impact
  4. Surfaces the simulation to affected companies as a notification
ALERT: WA Dept of Ecology published WAC 173-442 amendment (effective 2026-11-15).
       Your company (NAICS 238210, Electrical Contractor, 65% WA operations) is affected.
       Estimated impact: +$8,500/year compliance cost.
       Mitigation: 2 pathways identified — see simulator.
