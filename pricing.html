Pricing — Free Tier + Paid Tiers

Phase 1 wedge is free (3 tenders/day, 5 matrices/month, $99/bid-draft add-on). Phase 2 platform: $499 Pro / $2,400 Team / $6,000 Enterprise / $2,000 API-only.

Phase 1 free + $99/bidPhase 2 $499-$6KNo dark patternsBottom-up adoption

1 The Free-Tier Strategy

The wedge (Module B — Procurement & Gazette Engine) is free. The wedge's job is to compound the moat (compliance knowledge graph, provenance records, Indigenous-nation partnerships), not to generate revenue. Charging for the wedge would reduce usage, which would reduce moat-compounding.

The conversion bridge: $99/bid-draft add-on

The free tier is genuinely free, with explicit limits. When a free user hits a limit and wants more, they have two paths: (a) pay-per-bid-draft at $99/draft (a procurement officer who has used the free tier for 60 days knows the OS saves them $2-4K per bid vs. hiring a consultant — $99 is a no-brainer), or (b) upgrade to the Pro tier at $499/month for unlimited everything.

2 Free-Tier Limits — Phase 1 vs Phase 2

The limits tighten between Phase 1 (the wedge) and Phase 2 (the platform). Phase 1 needs moat-compounding; Phase 2 needs revenue conversion. The tightening is a database migration, not a code change.

Why the limits tighten in Phase 2

A Phase-1 user who was doing useful work on 3 tenders/day will hit the 1-tender/day Phase-2 limit in 4 days. The OS surfaces a clear "Upgrade to Pro for unlimited" callout. The 90% of users who used free in Phase 1 either upgrade (to Pro at $499/mo) or downgrade to occasional use (free-tier demo mode).

5 Anti-Patterns (What We Will NOT Do)

  • No feature ransom. The free tier has all the features of the Pro tier — same daily digest format, same compliance matrix generator, same bid-drafting agent. The only difference is quantity, not capability. We do not lock features behind the paid tier.
  • No dark patterns. When the free user hits a limit, the OS surfaces a clear "You've used 5 of 5 compliance matrices this month. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited ($499/mo), or pay $99 for one more bid draft." The user makes an informed choice. We do not auto-subscribe; we do not trick.
  • No "free forever" tier that erodes paid conversion. The free tier is for evaluation and light use. It is not a substitute for paid. Phase 2's tightening (from 3/day to 1/day) enforces this.
  • No paid salesforce in Phase 1. The wedge is bottom-up — a single procurement officer adopts, then brings their team. A salesforce pushing top-down doesn't fit.
  • No cold outbound email. The OS does not email people who didn't sign up.
  • No partnership with data brokers. The OS does not buy lead lists; does not sell user data; does not embed tracking pixels.

7 The Decision (Adopted October 2026)

The free-tier limits and paid tier pricing documented here were formally adopted in October 2026 (per docs/architecture/14-thoughts-and-refinements.md §8, open question #1 — resolved). The decision: Balanced Phase 1 (3 tenders/day, 5 compliance matrices/month) + Tight Phase 2 (1/day, 1/month), with the $99/bid-draft add-on as the conversion bridge.

The principle behind the design: free-tier limits should be tighter when you need revenue, looser when you need moat-compounding. Phase 1 needs moat; Phase 2 needs revenue.

Full reasoning documented in docs/FREE-TIER-LIMITS.md (private source-of-truth repo).
